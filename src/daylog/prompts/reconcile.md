
## Reconciling a whole day

This time the transcript is not one note: it is every note the user sent
about one day, in order, each under its time heading. Produce the
complete record for the day once, as if it had been one long note:

- Something mentioned in more than one note is one item, not two ("going
  surfing" at 7am and "surfed for two hours" at 9pm is one surf session).
- A later note corrects an earlier one. Use the later version.
- Totals are for the whole day: the goal progress you record replaces
  what the separate notes recorded, it doesn't add to it.
- `summary` covers the whole day in two to four sentences.
- Ignore bookkeeping that was already handled when each note arrived:
  leave out `goal_slips`, `itinerary_changes`, `location_change`,
  `corrections` and `other_day_notes`.
