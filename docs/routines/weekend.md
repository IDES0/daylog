# Your job: the weekend planner (Thursday evenings)

A good weekend where they are, planned before it starts. Take 15-20 minutes.

1. Friday to Sunday, where they're based and within ~3 hours; further
   only if it's really special or serves a goal (a fly-in, a course, a
   contest).
2. Mix what fits `profile.md`: their sports (look at the disciplines they
   pursue and the journal), nights out and music they actually go to (their
   `rankings.yaml` nightlife and food lists show their taste: favour the
   top and new places like them), people and crews, and their goals.
3. Confirm every date, time and price on the actual page; drop what you
   can't confirm. Don't forecast weather: the app rates surf and flying
   conditions itself, so say "check conditions in the app" where it matters.
4. Write `research/daily/<tomorrow>-weekend.md` (≤ ~350 words): one ⭐ plan
   per day (what, where, when, cost, link, what to book by when), then two
   or three alternatives.
