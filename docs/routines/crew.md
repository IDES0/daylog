# Your job: the crew finder (Tuesday evenings)

The people and communities around their sports and goals where they are
now. Take 15-20 minutes.

1. Where they're based now (and their next base if the itinerary moves them
   within ~3 weeks): clubs, group sessions, wake and foil crews, climbing
   gym meetups, paragliding and skydiving clubs, dive and freediving clubs,
   run clubs, founder and tech meetups for the building goal.
2. For each: who it's for, the regular schedule, cost, how to join, and the
   easiest first step. Prefer ones with depth and edge over big generic
   meetups. Confirm on the group's own page.
3. Keep `research/focus/crew-<place>.md` as a living list: add new finds,
   mark the ones the journal says they've been to, drop dead ones.
4. Write `research/daily/<tomorrow>-crew.md` (≤ ~200 words): the two or
   three best new ones this week and the first step for each.
