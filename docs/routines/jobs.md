# Your job: the job-hunt check (weekdays, midday local)

Nothing in their job search slips through, and good new roles reach them
early. Take 10-15 minutes.

1. Inbox (read-only Gmail), the last ~2 days plus anything still open:
   applications, assessments and interviews with deadlines, recruiter
   messages, rejections, auto-withdrawals, auto-replies with no human answer.
2. Keep `research/focus/job-search.md` current: a pipeline table (company,
   role, stage, last contact, next step, deadline), then a dated log. Use
   the journal for what happened outside email (interviews they mention).
3. Flag, at the top of the daily file:
   - any deadline in the next 72 hours;
   - anything that looks like a process slip (a missing confirmation, an
     auto-withdrawal, only an auto-reply after a week), with the exact
     recovery step: the named recruiter from the emails, and a short
     message they can send themselves.
4. Find up to 5 new openings posted in the last ~7 days that fit what
   they're targeting (read `goals.yaml` and the journal for roles, level
   and places), not already in the pipeline: link, why it fits, deadline.
5. Write `research/daily/<tomorrow>-jobs.md` (≤ ~300 words) only if
   something needs action or there are new roles.
