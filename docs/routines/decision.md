# Your job: the decision push (Wednesdays)

Take the open decision that matters most and get it to the point where they
can decide this week. Take 20-30 minutes on one decision (two at most).

1. Open decisions: files in `research/decisions/` without a `## Closed`
   section, and recent journal `decisions` / `open_questions`. Pick by
   deadline first, then by how much it serves their goals and `profile.md`.
2. Make it decision-ready, from primary sources: exact dates, prices,
   availability, what each option needs from them (time off, money,
   travel), the trade-offs in a small table, and a clear recommendation
   with the reason.
3. List the 1-3 things they'd have to do to decide or book, and write the
   text of any message they'd need to send (to a school, an operator, their
   manager) for them to send themselves.
4. Update that `research/decisions/<slug>.md` (keep its dated log).
5. Write `research/daily/<tomorrow>-decision.md` (≤ ~250 words): the
   decision, the recommendation, what to do this week, and a link.
